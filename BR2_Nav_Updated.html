Business Registration Processes – South Dakota to Wyoming

South Dakota
  • Entity Types: Corporations, LLCs, LPs, LLPs, Nonprofits
  • Filing Agency: South Dakota Secretary of State – Business Services
  • Filing Fee: $150 (LLC)
  • Key Steps:
    • File Articles of Organization online via the SOS site
    • Appoint a Registered Agent
    • Register for sales tax with the Department of Revenue
    • File annual reports online
  • Registered Agent Required: ✅ Yes
  • Online Filing: ✅ Available
  • One-Stop Portal: ❌ Not available
  • AI Chatbot: ❌ Not available
  • Website Assessment: Advanced – Needs Some Improvements
  • Visit South Dakota Business Portal
